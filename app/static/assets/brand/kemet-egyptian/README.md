# Kemet Egyptian Visual System
Version: 1.1 — Nile / Earth / Pyramids / Felucca Hero

The approved Login hero composition is:
1. Earth at night with an Egypt-facing glow on the left.
2. Nile water across the lower field.
3. Pyramids on the horizon.
4. Felucca on the right.
5. Warm low sun at the right horizon.
6. Deep Lapis / Nile Midnight cinematic sky.
7. Very restrained Egyptian glyph texture.
8. Login content sits above the scene; the scene must remain visibly present.

Design principle: modern global technology identity, not a museum, tourism poster, or dense Pharaonic UI.

Color tokens are maintained in visual-system.css.
The hero is an original local SVG interpretation of the approved reference composition, so the UI has no external image dependency.

Cultural reference basis:
- The Metropolitan Museum of Art documents lotus/water lily symbolism around the rising sun, rebirth, and rejuvenation.
- The Met documents lotus and papyrus as heraldic plants associated with Upper and Lower Egypt and their use together in unification imagery.

Reference URLs:
https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/545284
https://www.metmuseum.org/de/essays/papyrus-in-ancient-egypt

Application rule: use original abstractions and geometry; do not copy museum artwork into product UI. Keep heritage cues restrained and subordinate to usability.
