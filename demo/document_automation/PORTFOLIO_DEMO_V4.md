# Kemet Document Intelligence — Commercial V4 Demo

**Synthetic portfolio demo — no customer data.**

## Client promise
Send business documents. Receive clean, validated, traceable data with a clear review queue.

## V4 client journey
1. Smart Intake — file coverage and support status.
2. Classification — supported invoice or explicit unsupported status.
3. Extraction — structured business fields.
4. Evidence — value-level source line and confidence where available.
5. Validation — required fields, arithmetic and duplicates.
6. Risk Center — actionable exceptions with severity.
7. Client Rules — deterministic customer-specific policies.
8. Readiness Gate — READY, REVIEW_REQUIRED or BLOCKED.
9. Delivery — executive report plus professional Excel, CSV and evidence.
